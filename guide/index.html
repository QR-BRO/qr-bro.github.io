User guide

Generator · Contact links · Verify · Compare · My account and save slots · Appearance and language · Credits

Generator

Open in QR BRO ↗

Enter a URL or text. The preview updates as you edit. Download a PNG for a single image or a ZIP for separate QR layers.

QR content

Paste the complete text you want a scanner to read.

Encoding

Choose error correction and a mask pattern. Higher correction leaves more room to recover damaged data but can increase density.

Margins

Set the quiet border in modules. Four clear modules help scanners locate the code. Noise, safe space and layer controls change the surrounding design.

Pixels & markers

Choose module shapes and style the corner markers separately. Scan the result after a shape change.

Colors

Choose the light and dark colors or invert them. Keep enough contrast for scanning.

Advanced options

Expand this group to control QR density, dimensions and perspective.

Version & pixel size

Limit the QR version range and adjust pixel size. If the content exceeds the version limit, shorten it or raise the maximum.

Transform

Adjust perspective on each axis. Verify the exported image after applying distortion.

Scale & aspect

Set preview zoom, canvas size, proportions and transparency. Use the JSON controls to export or reload editable settings.

Contact links

Open in QR BRO ↗

Choose a platform first. Enter the requested recipient, such as an international phone number, username or email. Add a message where supported. For group email fields, separate recipients with commas or spaces. Copy the generated link or choose Create QR to open it in the generator.

Verify

Open in QR BRO ↗

Upload, drop or paste a QR image. Read the decoded content and scan status. Review contrast, quiet zone and density, then copy the result or send it to the generator.

Image differences

Inspect which pixels changed between the original and processed image.

Image adjustments

Try resizing, grayscale, contrast, brightness or blur. Lock a setting to keep it during randomized attempts. Compare the new scan with the original.

Compare

Open in QR BRO ↗

Upload a target image and a source QR or use the current generator output as the source. The third preview maps differences between modules. You can enlarge either input and copy its decoded text.

Target processing

Adjust grayscale, contrast, brightness, blur and pixelation before comparing.

Grid alignment

Use the target or source as the shared grid reference. Choose independent alignment when the images have different margins. Manual controls adjust grid size and margin.

Grid appearance

Enable the grid, select the images it appears on and adjust line color and opacity.

Overlay settings

Enable the overlay and adjust blending and opacity to compare overlapping details.

Difference settings

Choose a color mode for matches, difference type or intensity. Select modules for closer inspection. Reorder selections or change their identifying colors.

Comparison statistics

Read the mismatch count and average brightness to assess the selected comparison.

Correction settings

Adjust the correction, preview it, highlight differences and download the correction image or mask.

My account and save slots

Open in QR BRO ↗

Use the numbered buttons to switch between ten local projects. Sign in to sync across devices. Create an account with email and password and confirm via the email link or code. Google and GitHub are also available. In your account you can edit your profile, manage sign-in methods and two-factor authentication, review sync conflicts, back up settings or sign out.

Appearance and language

Open in QR BRO ↗

Open the appearance button beside the save slots. Choose a language, theme and light or dark mode. Your language choice is remembered on this device.

Credits

Open in QR BRO ↗

Open Credits for creator links, libraries and acknowledgments. Terms of use are linked in the site footer.

Recommended workflow

  1. Open Contact links to create a link, or copy the text you want your QR code to contain.

  2. Open the generator and paste your link or text into the QR content field.

  3. Try different margins, colors and shapes until you like the code in the preview.

  4. Click the large Download PNG button below the preview to save your QR code as an image.
